Wood & Faux Wood Blinds

Classic warmth with precise light control. The perfect blend of beauty and function.

Natural Beauty, Lasting Quality

Hunter Douglas wood blinds bring the warmth of natural hardwood to your windows. Choose from a variety of wood species, stains, and finishes to complement your home's interior design.

For high-humidity areas or homes near the coast, our faux wood blinds offer the same beautiful look with enhanced durability and moisture resistance.

Key Features

  • Genuine hardwood or durable faux wood options
  • Wide range of stains, paints, and finishes
  • Multiple slat sizes: 1", 2", and 2.5"
  • Precise light control with tilting slats
  • Cordless and motorized lift options
  • Moisture-resistant faux wood for bathrooms
  • De-Light feature for enhanced light control

Popular Collections

Parkland Wood Blinds - Genuine hardwood with furniture-quality finishes.

EverWood Alternative Wood Blinds - Durable, moisture-resistant faux wood.
