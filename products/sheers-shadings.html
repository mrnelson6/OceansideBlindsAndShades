Sheers & Shadings

Soft light diffusion with a view. Transform harsh sunlight into a beautiful glow.

Beautifully Diffused Light

Hunter Douglas sheers and shadings are designed to transform harsh sunlight into soft, diffused light while maintaining your view to the outdoors. These innovative window treatments combine the beauty of a sheer curtain with the functionality of a shade.

The signature Silhouette and Pirouette collections feature soft fabric vanes that appear to float between two sheer fabric layers, creating a stunning visual effect while providing excellent light control and UV protection.

Key Features

  • Soft, diffused light throughout the day
  • Maintain your view while controlling glare
  • Superior UV protection for furniture and floors
  • Tilting vanes for precise light control
  • Available with PowerView motorization
  • Room darkening options available
  • Perfect for living rooms with a view

Popular Collections

Silhouette Window Shadings - Iconic design with S-shaped vanes suspended between sheers.

Pirouette Window Shadings - Soft fabric vanes attached to a single sheer backing.

Luminette Privacy Sheers - Vertical vanes ideal for sliding doors and large windows.
