Roller Shades

Clean, modern design with versatile light control. Perfect for any room in your home.

Simple Elegance, Modern Function

Hunter Douglas roller shades offer a sleek, streamlined look that complements any interior design style. With a wide selection of fabrics, colors, and opacities, you can achieve the perfect balance of light control and privacy.

From sheer fabrics that softly filter light to room-darkening options for bedrooms and media rooms, roller shades provide versatile solutions for every space in your home.

Key Features

  • Wide variety of fabrics, colors, and textures
  • Light filtering to room darkening options
  • Motorization available with PowerView
  • Clean, contemporary appearance
  • Easy operation with cordless or motorized lift
  • Energy-efficient fabric options
  • Child and pet safe designs
