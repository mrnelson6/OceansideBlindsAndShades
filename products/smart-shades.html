Smart & Motorized Shades

Automated convenience with smart home integration. Control your shades with a tap or your voice.

The Future of Window Coverings

Hunter Douglas PowerView Automation transforms your window treatments into smart devices. Control your shades from anywhere using the PowerView App, schedule scenes to adjust automatically throughout the day, or use voice commands with your favorite smart home system.

Perfect for hard-to-reach windows, large sliding doors, and anyone who wants the convenience of automated light control. Wake up gently with shades that open with the sunrise, or have them close automatically at sunset for privacy.

Key Features

  • Control via smartphone app from anywhere
  • Voice control with Alexa, Google Home, and Siri
  • Scheduled scenes that adjust automatically
  • Integrates with most smart home systems
  • Whisper-quiet operation
  • Battery-powered or hardwired options
  • Perfect for hard-to-reach windows
  • Child and pet safe - no cords

Smart Home Integration

Apple HomeKit - Control with Siri and the Home app.

Amazon Alexa - Voice control with Echo devices.

Google Home - Works with Google Assistant.

Control4, Crestron, Savant - Professional home automation integration.
