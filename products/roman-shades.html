Roman Shades

Soft fabric elegance in timeless folds. The perfect blend of style and sophistication.

Elegant Folds, Timeless Style

Hunter Douglas Roman shades bring the softness of drapery with the functionality of a shade. They fold into beautiful horizontal pleats when raised and lie flat when lowered for a clean, tailored look.

Available in a wide range of fabrics, from casual linens to luxurious silks, Roman shades add warmth and texture to any room. Choose from flat, hobbled, or looped styles to match your design aesthetic.

Key Features

  • Soft, cascading folds for elegant appearance
  • Extensive fabric selection including linens, silks, and cottons
  • Flat, hobbled, and looped fold styles
  • Light filtering and room darkening options
  • Cordless and motorized lift systems
  • Custom linings for light control and privacy
  • Perfect for living rooms, bedrooms, and dining areas
