Cellular Shades

Energy-efficient honeycomb design for superior insulation and year-round comfort.

Energy Efficiency Meets Beautiful Design

Hunter Douglas Duette honeycomb shades are the original cellular shade, engineered with unique honeycomb construction that traps air to help keep your home warmer in winter and cooler in summer.

Available in a stunning array of colors, textures, and pleat sizes, Duette shades offer the perfect combination of energy efficiency and style. They can reduce heat loss through windows by up to 50% and unwanted solar heat by up to 80%.

Key Features

  • Superior energy efficiency - reduce heating and cooling costs
  • Honeycomb cells trap air for insulation
  • Light filtering to room darkening options
  • Architella double-cell for maximum efficiency
  • Cordless, top-down/bottom-up, and motorized options
  • Sound absorption for quieter rooms
  • UV protection for furniture and floors

Popular Collections

Duette - The original honeycomb shade with industry-leading energy efficiency.

Duette Architella - Double-cell construction for maximum insulation.

Duette Vertiglide - Vertical orientation for sliding doors and wide windows.
